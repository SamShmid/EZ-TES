Last updated: November 1, 2026

Reporting a problem

Email samuelshmidman@gmail.com and include:

  1. What you were doing and what you expected to happen.
  2. What happened instead (a screenshot helps).
  3. Your app version and your device and operating system version.
  4. A debug log, if you can (see below).

Sending a debug log

  1. Open the side menu and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Export Debug Log and pick a time range.
  3. Read the confirmation. The log includes your code values, nicknames, template names, file names and work times, plus your device's operating system. Share it only with someone you trust to help with support.
  4. Confirm, then send the file by email or any other app in the share sheet. The app deletes its copy afterward.

If you would rather not share names, send the report without the log and describe the steps.

Before you contact us

  • Export a CSV first (Export screen) so you have a copy of your data.
  • Entries on the wrong day? Entries belong to the day they were recorded, in the time zone where they were recorded.
  • Garbled characters in Excel? Exports include a UTF-8 BOM. If you open the file from Excel's Open dialog, choose UTF-8 if it asks for an encoding.
  • Excel drops leading zeros (for example 0159 becomes 159). The file is correct; this is Excel's display. Format the column as Text.

Privacy
