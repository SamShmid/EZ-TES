EZ-TES Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

EZ-TES is a time tracking app made by Samuel Shmidman. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Summary

  • Everything you enter stays on your device.
  • We do not collect, receive, or sell any of your data.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

What the app stores

EZ-TES stores your project codes and nicknames, time entries, export templates, and settings in a database on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you use Settings → Delete All App Data, or when you uninstall the app.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose to export or share:

  • CSV exports contain your time entries and codes. You choose where they go (email, Files, another app).
  • Code import and template import read files you pick from your device.
  • Debug logs are optional and are created only when you ask for one. A debug log includes your code values, nicknames, template names, file names and work times, plus your device's operating system. The app asks you to confirm before sharing it, and deletes the log file afterward. Share it only with someone you trust to help with support.

Once you share a file, the app you share it with is responsible for it under its own policy.

Third parties

EZ-TES does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any other third-party service that receives your data.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos. It accesses files only when you pick one to import or choose where to share an export.

Children

EZ-TES is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions: email samuelshmidman@gmail.com, or see the support page.
